# La Chona 🎉

**Your party starts with an idea. La Chona helps turn it into a plan.**

La Chona is a personal AI assistant purpose built for event planning. It is designed to help you organize parties with friends, special events, and social gatherings—and find the right providers for drinks, venues, food, entertainment, and more.

## The problem

Planning a celebration means juggling ideas, budgets, and providers across messages, searches, and scattered notes. Even a small party can become a lot of work before anyone arrives.

## The idea

Tell La Chona what you want to celebrate. It helps turn your preferences into a clear event plan, with provider options that fit your needs.

The intended experience is simple:

1. **Describe your event.** Share the occasion, location, date, guest count, budget, and vibe.
2. **Find your providers.** Explore options for venues, food, drinks, entertainment, and other event needs.
3. **Bring the plan together.** Organize your choices and next steps so you can focus on celebrating.

### Example

> “Help me plan a birthday party for 30 friends in Guadalajara. I need a venue, tacos, drinks, and a DJ, all within my budget.”

The goal: a practical plan and a shortlist of relevant providers, with clear next steps. This illustrates the planned experience; it is not a live result.
